Limits

Read before acting on any of the above.

Monthly moves are mostly noise — but two in a row are not

The survey samples 65,000 households and reports significance at 68% confidence. A single 68,000 swing would be weak evidence. Back-to-back declines totalling 110,000 are harder to dismiss.

The sector ladder lags by a month

The full 16-industry table runs to August. September's six reported movers are shown separately rather than merged, because mixing reference periods in one ranking would mislead.

Tariffs were not the September story

September was the first full month after Canada's 8 September counter-tariffs. The losses landed in the public sector, not in trade-exposed industries. Manufacturing's 13,000 decline may be an early signal, but one month does not establish it.

Exposure is not job loss

Exposure scores measure what AI could do to a job's tasks, not what employers have done. Roughly 90% of AI-adopting Canadian firms report no staffing change.

Targets are political documents

250,000 AI jobs, 125,000 defence careers, $500B investment: announced objectives with no independent verification. Direction, not quantity.
